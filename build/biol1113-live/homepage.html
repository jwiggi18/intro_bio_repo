"Effective learning emerges from an instructor's ability to express care and intentionality for students, and to create an environment of social belonging."

The first week of this course is intentionally different from most science courses you've taken. Before we get into biology, we talk about how you learn: what the research says about growth mindset, about who belongs in science, and about what science actually is as a process. Students tell me every semester that this week changes how they approach not just this class, but school in general.

My videos start with about a minute of immersion material (something calming) before content begins. That's not an accident. Every structural choice in this course is intentional. I want your attention freed up for the ideas, not for figuring out what's due or where to find things.

How this course works
One page. One week. Every module has a single weekly page that links to everything: videos to watch, readings to complete, and assignments to submit. Work through it top to bottom. You never need to hunt for anything.
🎬
Watch
Short instructor videos (under 10 min each), each followed by a Learning Notes submission. Work through them in order and submit a photo of your notes after each one.
📖
Read
Specific sections of OpenStax Biology 2e, a free peer-reviewed textbook. Links go directly to the section. You will never be assigned a full chapter.
✍️
Submit
Learning Notes, take-home homework (some weeks), writing assignments/blog posts, and three exams. All due Sunday at 11:59 PM.
🔁
Retry
Notes are graded on completion, not correctness. If something didn't click the first time, rewatch the video or revisit the reading before you submit your notes.
