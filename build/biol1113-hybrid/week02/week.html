The Cell & Its Membrane

Module opens Monday  ·  All work due Sunday, August 30, by 11:59 PM

Objectives

By the end of this week, you will be able to:

  • Distinguish prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells by the location of their DNA and the presence or absence of membrane-bound organelles.
  • Distinguish ionic and covalent bonds, and explain how bond type determines whether a molecule is polar or nonpolar.
  • Describe the phospholipid bilayer's structure and explain why it makes the membrane selectively permeable to charged versus uncharged particles.
  • Compare passive and active transport in terms of energy requirement and direction relative to the concentration gradient.
  • Explain how the sodium-potassium pump establishes the gradient that powers secondary active transport, such as sodium-glucose cotransport.
  • Apply the concepts of gated ion channels, electrochemical gradients, and osmosis to explain how cholera causes severe dehydration and why oral rehydration therapy works.
