Learning and the Nature of Science

Module opens Monday  ·  All work due Sunday, August 23, by 11:59 PM

Objectives

By the end of this week, you will be able to:

  • Apply principles from the neuroscience of learning to identify effective study strategies for this course
  • Identify the major themes that unify all living systems and the characteristics shared by all living organisms
  • Distinguish between a scientific hypothesis, theory, and law — and explain the role of evidence in each
  • Describe how peer review and scientific consensus function as quality-control mechanisms in science
  • Evaluate everyday biological claims using criteria from the scientific method
