### Option 1 honest assessment

**Good:** Nothing moves. Nothing is cut. Lowest risk to a schedule you already built.
Week 2 keeps its full macromolecule inventory intact.

holiday in it and is the last week before Test 1.** That is the weakest point in this
option and it is not a small one.

### Option 2 honest assessment

**Good:** The causal chain is continuous. Polarity to bilayer happens inside one week
instead of being interrupted. Week 2 drops from 51 to 35 minutes, which is real relief
in your second week when students are still finding their footing. Week 4 holds at 38
minutes, essentially unchanged, which respects the Labor Day short week.

Moving nucleic acid chemistry to the genetics block is arguably an improvement rather
than a relocation. Section 14.2 teaches DNA structure there anyway, so the chemistry
lands where it gets used.

**Bad:** It adds 5 minutes to a genetics block that is already compressed from 4 weeks
into 3 (about 141 minutes into 3 weeks). That block is the tightest part of your
semester and this makes it slightly tighter.

Week 2 at 35 minutes may read as light next to Week 1 at 37 and Week 3 at 31+. That is
probably fine, but it is a visible drop from the current 51.

# What I would flag if you asked

Option 2 is stronger on the two things that actually matter here.

The first is the Labor Day week. Week 4 under Option 1 carries 44 minutes across 7
videos, ending with the cholera capstone, in a short week, immediately before the test
that covers it. That is the week most likely to break, and Option 1 makes it heavier
while Option 2 leaves it where it is.

The second is that the whole reason for this restructure is the causal chain. Option 1
puts four videos between "water is polar" and "and that is why a membrane exists,"
which is most of what the integration was meant to eliminate.

The strongest argument for Option 1 is that it requires no decisions about what leaves
Week 2. That is a real advantage when you are trying to ship by August 12.

A middle path exists if you want it: run Option 2's Week 2 and Week 4, but keep nucleic
acids in Week 2 instead of moving it. Week 2 becomes 40 minutes and 7 videos, still
lighter than today, and the genetics block is untouched. That gets most of Option 2's
benefit without touching Weeks 7 to 9.
